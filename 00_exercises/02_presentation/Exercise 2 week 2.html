An aligned multi-row equation

The total number of airline passengers over 12 years is 40363 thousand. To calculate the average number of passengers per year, we divide the total number of airline passengers by the 12 years. Then, \[ \begin{align} 12x &= 40363 \\ x &= \frac{40363}{12} \\ x &\approx 3363.58 \\ \end{align} \] is the sample mean.

A citation

The study by Pala and Ünlük (2022) used the Airpassengers dataset for their analyses. This dataset contains the monthly totals of international airline passengers from 1949 to 1960.

library(tidyverse)

# Reading the data
data <- data.frame(
  date = seq(as.Date("1949-01-01"), as.Date("1960-12-01"), by = "month"),
  passengers = as.numeric(AirPassengers)
)

# Making a plot
ggplot(data, aes(x = date, y = passengers)) +
  geom_line() +
  labs(
    title = "Monthly Airline Passenger Numbers, 1949–1960",
    x = "Year",
    y = "Passengers (thousands)"
  ) +
  theme_minimal()
